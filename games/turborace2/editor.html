    Plan view — drag a waypoint to move it, drag empty space to box-select, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, double-click the road to insert. Checkpoints, sectors, the grid and the AI line are placed with the Race tools and start out laid out for you. Shift-click adds to the selection; hold Shift while dragging to ignore snapping.
    Profile view — drag the upper/lower handle to set that edge's height (bank), the middle to raise the whole gate; with several waypoints selected the middle handle raises all of them.
    Diagnostics — click one to jump to the road it is about.
    Surface — pick one and drag along the road to paint it; the car's grip follows what you lay down.
